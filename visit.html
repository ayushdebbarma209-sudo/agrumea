Visit · Floridia, Sicilia

By appointment · Small groups

Come walk
the rows.

To understand the flavour of an orange, or the intensity of a jar, you need to stand where it was born. We invite you into the grove — not as a spectator, but as a guest at our table.

1h 30mEach experience
2–12Guests
Nov–AprCitrus season

Three ways to spend an afternoon

Choose your pace.

01 · Harvest1h 30m

The golden path (your basket)

Take a basket and pick citrus directly from the tree, learning how to recognise the perfect stage of ripeness. Everything you pick goes home with you.

What’s included

  • Guided walk through the rows
  • The story of our varieties
  • Organic growing practices
  • A basket of fruit to take home
02 · Make1h 30m

Flavour workshop (from plant to jar)

Prepare your own personal jar, complete with a handwritten label, and take it home to keep. Our kitchen team shares the small decisions that make a good preserve.

What’s included

  • Hands-on preserve making
  • Our kitchen techniques
  • A personalised jar
  • Recipe notes to take away
03 · Taste1h 30m

The citrus gathering (a sensory tasting)

A gastronomic journey in the shade of the trees or in our tasting room: warm bruschetta, local cheeses, our preserves, fresh juice and wines from nearby growers.

What’s included

  • Seasonal tasting menu
  • Local cheeses and bread
  • Fresh citrus juice
  • Sicilian wines

Before you come

A few practical things.

Experiences are private, by appointment, and adapted to the season. We will confirm the details by email before your visit.

Request a visit
Where
Seconda strada, c.da Piana, S. Croce sn — 96014 Floridia (SR), Sicily. We will send exact directions and parking notes.
When
November to April for citrus. Workshops and tastings can be arranged outside the main harvest by request.
Group size
Two to twelve guests. Larger groups and private lunches can be arranged.
Languages
Italian and English. Other languages on request.
What to bring
Comfortable shoes, a hat in summer, and a bag for the fruit you pick.
Access
The grove is on uneven ground. Tell us about access needs and we will plan a route that works.

A note from the table

We keep the table long.

Experiences are led by the family, not a tour company. You will walk the same rows we walk, taste the jars we opened that morning, and leave with a more personal sense of what Sicilian fruit can be.

If you are planning a celebration, a press visit or a small team day, write to us. We like an occasion.
